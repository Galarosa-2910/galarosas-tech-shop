About the Founder

John Marco Galarosa

Student and Technology Enthusiast

Galarosa's Tech Shop was created out of a passion for high-performance computing, clean desk setups, and dependable peripherals. As a student navigating demanding coursework and technical projects, I realized how essential it is to have reliable, budget-friendly tech gear that enhances productivity and gaming without breaking the bank.

Our mission is to help fellow students and digital creators build their ideal workstations. Every product we showcase is curated with durability, ergonomic comfort, and performance in mind.
